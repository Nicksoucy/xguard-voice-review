How to review a lesson

  • Open a lesson and listen to it from start to finish. The text follows the voice.
  • Click any word that sounds wrong. Choose whether it is mispronounced or the wrong word, and add a short note if it helps.
  • If a whole sentence reads badly, click the pencil at the end of it and type the sentence you would like to hear.
  • Press the Glitch button (or the G key) if the voice stutters or cuts out.
  • When the lesson sounds right, click Approve lesson. Your work saves automatically as you go.

Your notes are read by the production team, who fix the text or the pronunciation and send the lesson back to you. You only need to listen and point things out.
