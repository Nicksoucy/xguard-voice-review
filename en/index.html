How to review a lesson

  • Open a lesson and listen to it from start to finish. The text follows the voice.
  • Click any word that sounds wrong. Choose whether it is mispronounced or the wrong word. In the box, type how it should sound (for example: wair-house) or the right word, then click Fix.
  • If a whole sentence reads badly, click the pencil at the end of it and type the sentence you would like to hear. If the wording is fine but the voice says it badly, choose “Redo it”.
  • Press the Glitch button (or the G key) if the voice stutters or cuts out, then click “Redo this sentence”.
  • When the lesson sounds right, click Approve lesson. Your work saves automatically as you go. If your internet drops, your notes stay on this computer and are sent when you come back.

A fix robot applies your fixes and redoes the voice, usually within a few minutes (it waits until you have not sent anything for 3 minutes). The sentences it redid turn green: listen to them again, then click “Sounds right now”. A few changes, such as a change to a number, are checked by the team first; the lesson tells you when that happens.
